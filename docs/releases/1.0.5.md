# Tasktap 1.0.5

Usage appears beneath the camera only while the notch is active: hover, capture or review. Closing the notch fades out the meters and retracts the entire row, restoring the original camera footprint. Hidden meters cannot receive clicks or keyboard focus. Opening and closing keep the provider positions anchored, with Reduce Motion support.

Workspace toolbar indicators remain visible. Subscription detection and quota reporting are unchanged.

Requires Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. Existing workspaces, notes, history and provider logins are preserved. Community builds are ad hoc signed, not Apple notarized.
