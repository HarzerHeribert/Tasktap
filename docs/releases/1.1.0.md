# Tasktap 1.1.0

The workspace and notch now stay open independently. Opening the notch no longer hides the workspace or moves its live view out of the window. Terminal output, tabs and previews continue while you capture or review a task.

Closing the workspace window leaves the resident notch running. Click Tasktap in the Dock or choose Open Workspace to restore the same window and tabs. Quit Tasktap still exits the app.

Shared project, session, note and decision records synchronize between the two views. The workspace remains the owner of terminal connections and saved layouts. App updates check unfinished capture and submission in both views before restarting.

Keep `/Applications/Tasktap.app` in the Dock. Older project/build copies are development artifacts and do not need to be pinned.

Requires Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. Existing workspaces, notes, history and logins are preserved. Community builds are ad hoc signed, not Apple notarized.
